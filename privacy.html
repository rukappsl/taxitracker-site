TaxiTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Operated by RUK APPSL Limited, New Zealand

TaxiTracker records the distance you drive so you can claim vehicle expenses in your tax return. To do that it needs your location. This policy explains exactly what is collected, why, where it is kept, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. Your trips and expenses are yours. We do not sell them, we do not advertise, and the app contains no analytics or tracking software of any kind. The only companies that touch your data are the ones that host it for us, listed below.

Who we are

TaxiTracker is operated by RUK APPSL Limited, a company registered in New Zealand (NZBN 9429053924827), based in Auckland. For anything in this policy, contact rukappsl@gmail.com. Our privacy officer can be reached at the same address.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your email address
  • A password, which is stored only as a cryptographic hash — we never see or store the password itself

Location, while a trip is running

When you tap Start trip, the app records your position periodically until you tap Stop trip. This continues while the app is closed or your phone is locked, which is the whole point — a driver cannot hold a phone while driving.

From that we store the total distance travelled, and the coordinates of where the trip started and finished. We do not store the full route.

The app never collects your location when a trip is not running.

What we do not collect

  • No contacts, photos (other than receipts you choose to attach), messages, or call history
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, usage tracking, crash reporting or behavioural profiling
  • No payment card details — if the app is ever paid for, the app store handles that and we never see your card

Where your data is stored

Your account and records are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, on servers in Sydney, Australia. Receipt photographs are held in private storage in the same place.

Database access rules mean each account can only ever read and write its own rows. One driver cannot see another driver's trips, expenses or receipts.

Some data is also held on your own phone so the app works without a connection — a trip recorded in a coverage gap is saved on the device and uploaded when signal returns. Your login token is kept in the phone's encrypted keystore.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. It is shared only with the services that make the app work:

  • Supabase — hosts the database, accounts and receipt storage
  • Expo — delivers app updates. This involves your device requesting updates from Expo's servers, which necessarily reveals your IP address and basic device type. It does not involve your trips, expenses or location.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store — distribute the app, and handle any payment if the app is ever paid for. We never receive your card details.

We will disclose data if we are legally required to, for example under a court order. If that ever happens and we are permitted to tell you, we will.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed — see below.

Note that Inland Revenue expects tax records to be kept for seven years. We do not keep your records that long on your behalf. Exporting the year-end Excel file and storing it yourself is how you meet that obligation — the app gives you that export at any time.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may:

  • See your data — it is all visible in the app, and the Reports tab exports the lot to a spreadsheet
  • Correct it — every trip and expense can be edited in the app
  • Delete it — see below
  • Complain — to us first at rukappsl@gmail.com, and if you are not satisfied, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, every trip and expense, your receipt photographs, and your settings. It cannot be undone, so export your records first if you need them.

If you cannot use the app, request deletion at this page or email rukappsl@gmail.com.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit
  • Database rules restrict every account to its own records
  • Receipt photographs are in private storage, not publicly reachable
  • Your login token is stored in your phone's encrypted keystore

If a privacy breach occurs that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, as the Privacy Act 2020 requires.

Children

TaxiTracker is for working drivers and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

TaxiTracker is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice. Always confirm figures, categories and the current IRD kilometre rate with your accountant or at ird.govt.nz before filing.
