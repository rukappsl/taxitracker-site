Delete your TaxiTracker account

Operated by RUK APPSL Limited, Auckland, New Zealand

You can delete your TaxiTracker account and all of its data at any time. There are two ways to do it — inside the app, or by emailing us if you no longer have the app installed.

Export your records first if you need them. Inland Revenue expects tax records to be kept for seven years. Deletion is permanent and we cannot recover anything afterwards — so generate the year-end Excel file from the Reports tab and save it somewhere before you delete.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open TaxiTracker and go to Settings
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account
  3. Confirm twice — the second prompt is the point of no return

Deletion is immediate.

Option 2 — by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email rukappsl@gmail.com from the address your account is registered under, with the subject Delete my account.

We will confirm the request, delete the account, and reply to confirm — normally within a few working days, and no later than 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Every trip — dates, times, distances, coordinates, notes and fares
  • Every expense, including amounts, categories and notes
  • All receipt photographs
  • Your settings, saved locations and odometer readings

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is retained. Deletion removes your account and records permanently.

If we are legally required to retain something specific, we will tell you what and why when we confirm your deletion.
